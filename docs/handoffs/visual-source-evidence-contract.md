- robe WALK: 20 rows (NW/NE/SW/SE x IDLE+4 WALK), alpha bounds + X/Y registration + foot/center result;
- attack BODY: 4 actual mm001 group-02 rows, normalized with translation/crop, common scale 1.70, height/foot/center errors;
- attack layers: 4 rows requiring BODY + mu0000058 FULL_BODY + mw001 atomic when equipped and no static-IDLE weapon-only swing;
- mw001: 4 rows with independent mirror, actual source blade-tip transform and hand anchor.
